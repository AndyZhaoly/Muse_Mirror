# Occasion guide

- **Interview / important business:** credible, neat, low-distraction, and at least as formal as the environment. Industry context matters.
- **Client meeting:** polished and trustworthy; avoid a footwear or bag choice that makes the outfit substantially more casual than the clothing.
- **Commute:** comfort plus clarity. Creative workplaces can support one stronger style element.
- **Date:** natural, intentional, and personally expressive with one memorable detail; avoid looking costume-like.
- **Wedding / banquet:** follow the invitation or host dress code first; do not imitate a real product or ceremonial role inaccurately.
- **Travel / outdoor:** weather, walking, layering, and care requirements come first.
- **Party:** one or two focal points are usually enough; let the rest support them.
- Context includes venue, time, industry, climate, activity, and the user's own presentation—not only the event label.
