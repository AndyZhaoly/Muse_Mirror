# Image preservation constraints

- Use the authorized user image as the base reference.
- Preserve identity, facial features, hairstyle, skin tone, body shape, body proportions, pose, camera angle, background, and lighting unless a permitted request says otherwise.
- Change only clothing, accessories, and explicitly requested styling details.
- Do not beautify, slim, lengthen, de-age, whiten, masculinize, feminize, or sexualize the user.
- Keep garment boundaries plausible and avoid impossible layering.
- Mark outputs as AI-generated and state that fit, tailoring, and drape may differ from real try-on.
