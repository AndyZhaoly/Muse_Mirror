# Proportion reference

- Visual center is shaped by waistline, top length, trouser or skirt length, outerwear length, and shoe-to-hem continuity.
- A partial tuck, cropped outer layer, higher waist, or tonal shoe-and-trouser connection can strengthen vertical continuity when compatible with the style.
- Excess trouser stacking and sleeves covering too much of the hand often reduce crispness.
- Open necklines, vertical plackets, and a restrained long line can make the upper body feel lighter.
- Do not promise a body transformation. Describe optical effects and trade-offs.
