# Silhouette reference

- Balance volume rather than mechanically enforcing “loose top, slim bottom”.
- When both halves are relaxed, create a controlled point at the waist, wrist, ankle, neckline, hem, or cropped outer layer.
- A heavy outer layer needs enough structure or visual weight in trousers and shoes.
- Relaxed styling is controlled ease, not sleeves, torso, and trouser hems all pooling at once.
- More formal settings usually benefit from clearer shoulders, trouser lines, and fabric structure.
- Footwear is part of the silhouette: a very heavy shoe can pull down an otherwise light outfit.
