# Evaluation rubric

A complete outfit should normally identify a top, bottom or one-piece, footwear, and any outer layer required by weather or formality.

Suggested internal thresholds:

- no major dress-code mismatch;
- no uncontrolled competing color blocks;
- no unresolved volume imbalance or excessive pooling;
- footwear supports the formality and visual weight;
- practical constraints are satisfied;
- explicit dislikes and comfort needs are respected.

Hard issues include unsafe weather mismatch, recommending a prohibited item, presenting an AI image as a real product, or asking the user to buy an entire wardrobe when they requested an owned-item solution.
